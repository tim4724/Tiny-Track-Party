CHANGES MADE. The audio content of every track is unaltered — no edits, no
filters, no loudness normalisation baked into the file (the per-song trim is
applied at PLAYBACK, from musicCatalogue.js). What we did change is the
ENCODING: each track ships re-encoded from the incompetech download to ~130
kbps VBR MP3, and the embedded cover art four of them carried is stripped.
Tracks are looped in game.

CC-BY 4.0 asks that modifications be indicated, so this note is the indication
and must stay accurate. The masters are NOT in this repo — SOURCES.json beside
this file records each track's download URL and the sha256 of the master it was
encoded from, and `npm run fetch:music` rebuilds the whole set from them.
